🧠 How to Study

Marty Lobdell shared two insightful quotes:

"If it doesn’t change your behavior, you haven’t learned it."

"Teachers want students to achieve. It drives them."

📚Study Place

Where should I study?

ℹ️ A specialized study space includes a desk and adequate lighting.

⚠️ A bedroom is for sleeping. Kitchen/dining rooms are for cooking and eating. Avoid studying in these rooms.

⏱️Study Time

How should I manage my study time effectively?

⏱️ Enhance your learning efficiency. Focused study for 25 minutes. Followed by a 5-minute break.

🗝️ Key Concepts for studying

Grasping the distinction between facts and concepts is essential.

Facts are fixed data requiring memorization.

Concepts are more expansive and meaningful.

🧠 Recognition vs. Recollection

ℹ️ Know the difference between recognition and recollection.

Recognizing an old magazine is different from recalling its content sequence and ads

☑️Recollection entails retaining both the information and the comprehensive understanding of a topic.

📖 Textbooks and SQ3R

SQ3R is a method for effective reading and comprehension.

ℹ️ Textbooks are powerful tools, but students need to learn how to use them.

One such approach is SQ3R.

SQ3R stands for:

Survey, Question, Read, Recite, and Review.

SQ3R Method

How to use SQ3R for effective reading:

🔍 Survey

Before diving into the reading, take a moment to scan the pages.

Pay attention to photos, bold or italic text, quiz questions, and text queries.

❓ Question

If there are quiz questions provided, review them first. This will help you focus on key points.

Take note of any bold text or images and ask yourself why they might be significant in the context of the textbook.

📖 Read

Read the chapter with full attention to make connections and find deeper meaning. Discuss the "bigger picture" and the concepts you've learned.

Share your knowledge with someone or even an 🪑 empty chair — after all, we are storytellers.

📝 Review

Always revisit your work, especially before a test. Connect what the teacher highlights in class with your textbook readings and lecture notes.

Ask your instructor for help and attend office hours.

Remember: "Teachers are driven by their students' success."

🧪 Test Preparation

⏱️When preparing for a test, we all have the same amount of time. How you use it is what matters.

⚖️ It's about balancing opportunity cost and value. If you're involved in one activity, you can't do another activity. So, understand what's valuable.

☑️ Start studying for a test right from the beginning of the quarter or semester using effective techniques.

☑️ Ensure you get enough sleep and avoid cramming the night before the test!

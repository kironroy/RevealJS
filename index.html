📚Study Place

Where should I study?

ℹ️ A specialized study space includes a desk and adequate lighting.

⚠️ A bedroom is for sleeping. Kitchen/dining rooms are for cooking and eating. Avoid studying in these rooms.

⏱️Study Time

How should I manage my study time effectively?

⏱️ Enhance your learning efficiency. Focused study for 25 minutes. Followed by a 5-minute break.

🗝️ Key Concepts for studying

Grasping the distinction between facts and concepts is essential.

Facts are fixed data requiring memorization.

Concepts are more expansive and meaningful

🧠 Recognition vs. Recollection

ℹ️ Know the difference between recognition and recollection.

Recognizing an old magazine is different from recalling its content sequence and ads

☑️Recollection entails retaining both the information and the comprehensive understanding of a topic.

📖 Textbooks and SQ3R

SQ3R is a method for effective reading and comprehension.

Textbooks are powerful tools, but students need to learn how to use them.

One such approach is SQ3R. SQ3R stands for: Survey, Question, Read, Recite, and Review.
